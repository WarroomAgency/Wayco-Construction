A new ADU in San Diego County typically costs several hundred thousand dollars to build. Wayco Construction's turnkey packages for a 1,200 sq ft, 2 bedroom, 1 bathroom ADU run from $325,000 to $379,000. Site conditions, utility connections, and finish selections move the final price, so a firm number comes after we see your property.

## What does a turnkey ADU package actually include?

When we say "turnkey," we mean you are not coordinating a dozen separate trades. Our ground-up ADU packages include city and county pre-approved design plans, full engineering, complete construction, appliances and fixtures, and a move-in-ready finish. Wayco handles all required permitting.

That matters because the cheapest-looking bid is often the one that leaves things out. Before you compare numbers, make sure every quote lists the same items: design, engineering, permits, site work, utility connections, appliances, flooring, and landscaping or hardscape restoration.

## What drives the cost of an ADU in San Diego?

Five things move the number more than anything else.

### How big is the ADU and how complex is the layout?

Size is the obvious driver, but layout matters as much. Kitchens and bathrooms are the most expensive rooms per square foot because of plumbing, electrical, cabinetry, and tile. A 1,200 sq ft ADU with a single bathroom and a compact kitchen costs less than a same-size plan with extra wet rooms or a complex roofline.

### What are the site conditions?

Slope, soil, drainage, tree roots, and equipment access all affect the foundation and the schedule. A level lot with a clear side yard is straightforward. A hillside lot, a tight alley, or a backyard that requires hand-carrying material costs more. We walk the property before we commit to a price for this reason.

### How far are the utilities?

Your ADU needs power, water, and sewer. If the connections sit close to the planned location, the work is simple. If the sewer line is far from the build site, or your electrical panel needs an upgrade, expect added cost. Utility agency requirements vary, so check with your city or county and your utility providers early.

### What finishes do you choose?

Cabinets, countertops, flooring, tile, fixtures, and windows are where the budget stretches or tightens. Our packages include appliances and fixtures, and we can talk through upgrades before you commit. If you want to see materials in person, our showroom is open by appointment, and showroom customers receive discounts on materials.

### Which type of ADU are you building?

Detached, attached, and garage-conversion ADUs carry different cost profiles. A conversion reuses an existing structure. A detached unit needs a new foundation, framing, and roof. We cover that tradeoff in [our guide to detached, attached, and garage-conversion ADUs](/blog/detached-attached-or-garage-conversion-adu).

## How do ADU cost drivers compare?

| Cost driver | Lower-cost scenario | Higher-cost scenario |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lot and access | Flat lot, open side yard | Slope, tight access, retaining needed |
| Utilities | Connections near the build site | Long runs, panel upgrade, sewer distance |
| Layout | One bathroom, compact kitchen | Extra wet rooms, complex roofline |
| Finishes | Standard package selections | Custom tile, upgraded cabinetry |
| ADU type | Garage conversion | Detached ground-up build |

## How should you set an ADU budget?

Start with three numbers: what you can spend, what the project is likely to cost, and what you want in reserve. A reserve for surprises is not pessimism, it is how well-run projects stay calm.

- Ask for a written scope that lists inclusions and exclusions.
- Ask what could change the price once work begins, and how changes are approved in writing.
- Confirm who handles permits and agency submittals.
- Ask about the warranty. Ours is a 1-year workmanship warranty.
- Get your financing path sorted out before design starts, not after.

## Will an ADU pay for itself?

That depends on your goals, and we are not financial advisors. Some owners build for family, some for rental income, some for both. Rental rates, financing terms, and property tax effects vary, so talk to a lender and a tax professional about your specific situation before you rely on any return estimate.

## What questions should you ask before you compare ADU bids?

Two bids for the same ADU can differ by tens of thousands of dollars without either contractor being dishonest. The gap usually hides in what each one assumed. Put the same questions to everyone you talk to.

- Does the price include design, engineering, and every required permit?
- Are utility connections, trenching, and any panel upgrade included or excluded?
- Which appliances, flooring, and fixtures are included, and which are allowances?
- Who is responsible for landscaping, fencing, or driveway restoration after construction?
- What is the process and pricing for changes once work has started?

A contractor who answers these clearly, in writing, is giving you something more useful than a low number. They are showing you how the project will be run.
